# How to test: Margyn helps from the first minute (after the merge)

This is the investor path: a **brand-new account** connecting its **own** Zoho Books. Takes about 15 minutes, plus one check an hour later.

You need: a new email address (e.g. varad+inv1@margynlabs.com) and a Zoho Books login (your Zoho test org is fine).

## Part 1 — a new account's first ten minutes
1. Open a private (incognito) window → **www.margynlabs.com/app.html** → **Sign up** with the new email.
   - ✅ The Margyn panel opens by itself and says **"Let's get your books in"**, with **Zoho Books / Tally / Odoo** buttons.
2. Press **Zoho Books** → **Continue to Zoho** → sign in → **Accept** → pick your organisation → **Confirm organization**.
   - ✅ The Margyn panel says **"I'm reading your books from Zoho now."**
   - ✅ Under it, a grey box counts up, e.g. **"Reading your invoices… 1,000 so far"**, then ticks **"✓ 1,204 invoices"**.
3. Wait (1–3 minutes on most books).
   - ✅ **"Zoho Books is connected"**, then **"Here's your first read"**: cash, who owes you most, most late customer, bills due this week.
   - ✅ The figures match Zoho's own **Reports → Receivables / Payables** (who owes most, total overdue).
4. Margyn asks **"What's the least cash you never want to go below?"** → type **5 L** → **Save**.
   - ✅ "Saved. I'll warn you if cash looks set to drop below ₹5 L."
5. Margyn lists your biggest customers and asks for WhatsApp numbers.
   - Put **your own mobile** next to the first customer (so a test reminder comes to you, not a real customer) → **Save numbers**.
   - ✅ "Saved 1 number to your customers. Nothing goes to them unless you tap Send."
   - (If your Zoho contacts already have numbers, Margyn just says so and moves on.)
6. Margyn asks **who should get the morning update** → type your mobile → **Send it to me**.
   - ✅ "Done. Your morning update goes to +91 … on WhatsApp."
   - ✅ **"That's you set up."**
7. Press **Skip** is fine on any question: try it on a second test account if you like.
   - ✅ It moves to the next question; nothing breaks.

## Part 2 — coming back later
8. Close the tab. Come back **after at least 30 minutes** (or the next morning) → open the app → open the Margyn panel.
   - ✅ A message **"In your books since <day>:"** with money that came in and from whom, what went overdue, bills due this week.
   - ✅ Buttons: **Remind <customer>**, **Send this week's payables to my accountant** (only if bills are due), **Show me**.
9. Press **Remind <customer>** (the one whose number you set to your own mobile).
   - ✅ Margyn shows the **exact WhatsApp message** first, and a **Send to …1234** button. Nothing is sent yet.
10. Press **Send to …1234**.
    - ✅ "Sent to <customer> on WhatsApp." and the reminder arrives on **your** phone.
11. Press **Send this week's payables to my accountant**.
    - ✅ A box with the list of bills → **Open in WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the text (you pick the contact and press send yourself).
12. Press **Show me**.
    - ✅ The list of who owes you opens in the panel.

## Make sure nothing else changed
13. Open **Mihir's account** (Care Hygiene, Tally) as usual.
    - ✅ Same greeting as before. No "first read", no questions, no "In your books since…".

## Clean up
14. On the test account: **Organisations and sources → Zoho Books → Disconnect** (so your Zoho org isn't read twice every night).
