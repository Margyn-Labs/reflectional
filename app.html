Books

Your books, every source

Every accounting source in one place. Where two sources agree on a figure, Margyn treats it as verified and it carries your Pulse Score; where only one reports it, the score still uses it but says so. Disagreements are flagged, never averaged.
Showing every connected accounting source. Margyn never adds these together. For each scoring figure it takes the strongest source available — two that agree beat one, and one connected source beats a number typed by hand — and flags anything that disagrees rather than averaging it.

Sources that disagree

Margyn used the stronger source for the score and left the difference here rather than splitting it. Cash from Tally and Odoo is the books' own bank and cash-in-hand total, with overdraft and cash-credit accounts left out — an interim signal until a bank feed replaces it. If two sources disagree on cash, that gap is usually uncleared items, not an error in either.
