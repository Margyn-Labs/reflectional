The product

All your money, in one clear picture.

Your books, gateways and store stay exactly where they are. Margyn reads them all, puts the numbers together, and points out where they don't match. Nothing to install for your team, nothing to type in twice.

Ask it the way you'd ask your accountant.

"Who should I chase first?" Margyn answers from your real numbers, in seconds, and tells you honestly when a figure comes from only one system.

  • Same answers in the app and on WhatsApp
  • Explains numbers, never invents them
  • Anyone on your team can ask, not only finance

See exactly where your systems disagree.

Put Tally and Zoho Books side by side. Figures that match are marked Verified. Figures that don't show the exact gap, so you fix them before they cost you.

  • One combined view, any single system, or both side by side
  • Gaps are flagged, never averaged away
  • Click any row to see where it came from

Spot a cash crunch weeks before it hits.

A 13-week forecast built from what customers owe you, what you owe vendors, and GST. Tell it how late your customers really pay, and it adjusts.

  • Set a minimum cash level and get warned
  • Every assumption written in plain words
  • Uses the same numbers as your Home screen

Your monthly report, already written.

Cash, receivables, payables, GST and what changed, with a short written summary. Save it as a PDF for your board, your bank or your CA.

  • Ready as soon as your books are
  • Anything written by AI is clearly marked
  • Every figure links back to its source

Where Margyn is going

Every step up is off until you switch it on, and every step follows the same rule: two systems must agree, and you approve.

Live todayReads and explains

Coming nextUpdates your books

Coming laterMoves money for you

What Margyn will never do

  • Average two systems that disagree
  • Act without your approval
  • Hold your money itself
  • Sell your data or score you for lenders
  • Message your customers without your OK
